Publishing without connector dependence

One adapter failing is not the fabric failing.
First-party/native routes, direct official APIs, connector adapters and owner handoff routes are qualified independently. The source-level first-party substrate can materialize content-addressed manifests plus JSON Feed and Atom projections. Quota exhaustion preserves the durable job; it never authorizes identity rotation, spoofing, rate-limit evasion or a fake “published” state. SOURCE_READY is not HOSTED or VERIFIED_REACHABLE.
