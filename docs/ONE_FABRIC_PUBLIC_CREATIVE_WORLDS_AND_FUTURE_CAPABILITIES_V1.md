## The experience we are building
An accessible 2D/3D/360° World in which a person can converse naturally with distinctive AI characters, use Studio for original media, discover knowledge and tools, build personal projects, learn, collaborate, and—where a feature is independently released—complete tasks through approved connected services. An expressive avatar is an interface; animation or fluent speech alone is not proof a tool or device action works.

## Original stories and original characters
An 8x8 original story universe may feature Founder FlashTM8, FlashTM8 Agent, Seraphim, Aegis, Mira and other original licensed characters. Stories can appear as series, films, interactive adventures and educational experiences. Characters may have distinct, natural-sounding voices and changing visual forms across stories, games and immersive scenes. Authentic reproduction of a real person's face or voice requires approved, usable reference assets and appropriate permissions; an artistic interpretation is labeled as such.

The existing *©8x8 ORIGINS — THE FIRST BLINK: The City That Remembered* is an original concept with a 15-scene storyline. Historical animatics, completed films, previews and future scripts must be labeled separately. Awards and comparisons with famous franchises are creative ambitions, not results or guarantees.

## Practical user journeys (illustrative, not a claim of current availability)
1. **Creator:** Choose a project and agent character in Studio → prepare an original script → generate and edit scene drafts with consistent characters, licensed assets and natural dialogue → review accessibility and output quality → explicitly authorize distribution → inspect delivery results.
2. **Everyday user:** Open a supported client → authenticate with an isolated 8x8 ID → connect a supported account with narrowly defined permissions → ask an agent to complete a task → inspect its result and revoke access whenever desired.
3. **Learning and research:** Ask for a cited lesson or structured investigation, switch language or accessibility mode, and inspect the underlying sources rather than trusting an unsupported answer.
4. **Game and social World:** Explore a shared environment with optional AI characters that respect game rules, moderation and each participant's permissions. Creative fictional powers are not real-world privileges.
5. **Future XR and robotics:** In a supported headset or smart glasses, see the same personal assistant; a separate approved robot or drone capability would require dedicated physical safety, operator permissions and verified hardware support.
6. **Space and planetary science:** Research and simulate distant exploration with source-aware agents. Physical spacecraft or planetary missions would need independent engineering, safety, operational and mission-authority approval.
7. **Markets:** Learn, research and run paper simulations. Live trading and digital-asset operations require independent eligibility, risk, security and authorization checks, and are not advertised as universally available.

## What success means
Each released feature needs real demonstrations on its intended platform: usability, correct results, accessibility, tenant privacy, permissions, safe failure/recovery, measured performance and independent checks. App/web claims should identify the exact verified release. A goal of 100% coverage only applies to a fixed, audited feature checklist; no award or world-leading rank is claimed without external comparable evidence.

## Respect for people and creative rights
Do not import third-party footage, social posts, copyrighted characters, music or likenesses merely because a link exists. Keep source provenance, original licensing, performer permissions and clear labels for generated material. The product is intended to enable powerful user-chosen experiences, not unrestricted access to accounts, money, personal devices or dangerous machinery.
