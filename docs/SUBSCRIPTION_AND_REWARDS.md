# Subscription and Rewards Boundary

## Current status

- Target monthly subscription: **USD 8.88**
- Billing live: **No**
- Recurring charge authorized: **No**
- Token purchase required for subscription: **No**
- Node rewards live: **No**
- Token or coin issuance authorized by this repository: **No**

The price is a product target until a production billing system, terms, cancellation flow, support process, tax treatment, and payment receipts are verified.

## Subscription requirements

A future paid plan must show, before purchase:

- the exact amount and currency;
- that the charge recurs monthly;
- the first charge date;
- renewal timing;
- included allowances and material limits;
- taxes or additional fees when applicable;
- how to cancel;
- when cancellation becomes effective;
- refund policy;
- support and dispute route.

Enrollment requires a separate affirmative action. A trial, token claim, wallet connection, node enrollment, sponsor payment, or free-account creation must not silently create a paid subscription.

Cancellation must be available through a clear product flow and must produce a receipt. The system must not hide recurring terms in secondary text or make cancellation materially harder than enrollment.

## Entitlements

Subscription entitlement and node contribution are separate.

A user must be able to subscribe without contributing device resources, precise location, credentials, storage, compute, bandwidth, mining hardware, or telemetry. A user must also be able to stop resource contribution without losing already purchased subscription time, except where a clearly disclosed contribution-specific benefit legitimately ends.

## Rewards research

The long-term concept includes eight ecosystem tokens and one 8x8 coin. This is **RESEARCH_ONLY**.

Before any live reward or asset issuance, the project requires at least:

- finalized utility and non-duplicative purpose for each asset;
- legal classification and offering analysis by jurisdiction;
- tax and accounting design;
- sanctions and restricted-jurisdiction controls;
- consumer-protection review;
- custody and key-management decision;
- market-manipulation and insider controls;
- smart-contract audit and upgrade policy;
- issuance, supply, treasury, vesting, and governance rules;
- anti-Sybil and fraud controls;
- dispute, reversal, slashing, and incident policies;
- public risk disclosures;
- independent economic simulations;
- exact owner and release approvals.

## Reward accounting stages

Any future contribution record must use one of these explicit stages:

1. `SIMULATED` — test data only, no entitlement or monetary value.
2. `MEASURED_UNPRICED` — verified resource contribution with no reward value assigned.
3. `OFFCHAIN_CREDIT_PENDING` — proposed credit subject to review and dispute.
4. `OFFCHAIN_CREDIT_FINAL` — finalized platform credit, not a token or cash claim unless terms say otherwise.
5. `ONCHAIN_SETTLEMENT_PENDING` — gated transaction prepared but not broadcast.
6. `ONCHAIN_SETTLED` — transaction independently verified on the designated chain.

No earlier stage may be described as money earned, a token balance, investment return, APY, mining profit, or guaranteed reward.

## Marketing limits

Public materials must not promise:

- guaranteed profit or token appreciation;
- a fixed APY without a lawful, measured, and fully disclosed product;
- risk-free participation;
- guaranteed liquidity or exchange listing;
- automatic income from storage, CPU, GPU, GPS, ASICs, or mining rigs;
- that subscribing is an investment;
- that another AI or institution recommends purchasing or investing unless a public source proves it and any relationship is disclosed.

## Separation of payments and assets

The USD 8.88 subscription should initially use ordinary payment rails and accounting. Token or coin participation must be optional and separately consented. Subscription access must not depend on speculative asset purchases.
