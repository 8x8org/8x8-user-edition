# 8x8 One-Fabric Eight-Gate Contract V1

Canonical root: `fabric://8x8/core`

MSG331 replaces any five-gate completion wording for MSG329/MSG330-era One-Fabric work with an explicit eight-gate engineering contract. Five triggered repository workflows are not the same thing as the eight canonical evidence gates.

## The eight gates

1. **VERITAS** — deterministic software/math invariants pass.
2. **REGISTRY** — normalization, deduplication, coverage-state and metric invariants pass.
3. **64-CELL TOPOLOGY** — exactly eight canonical families and the 8×8/64-cell contract remain valid.
4. **TRUTH BOUNDARY** — open-world/global 100% cannot be asserted from a bounded denominator.
5. **RECEIPT INTEGRITY** — canonical receipts are deterministic SHA-256 values.
6. **PROVENANCE** — every capability record carries source, revision and a valid provenance hash.
7. **PARITY EVIDENCE** — PARITY/SUPERIOR require test and receipt evidence; declaration alone is insufficient.
8. **ROOT LOCK** — all canonical capability records validate under `fabric://8x8/core` and the eight-family row mapping.

## Repository quality workflows

Repository-wide security/quality workflows such as Public Information Boundary, SBOM/Provenance, Browser Quality Gates and CodeQL remain additional enforcement surfaces. They do not reduce the canonical eight-gate model to five merely because five workflows happened to trigger on a particular PR.

## Completion rule

A work unit may report `8/8 PASS` only when all eight canonical gates execute against the exact candidate head and pass. Repository-wide workflows that trigger for that head must also pass before merge. An open-world denominator remains open even after `8/8 PASS`.

## Scope

This contract is software/evidence infrastructure. It does not certify speculative physical claims, automatic native pickup, all historical external capabilities, every connector, every file, or global whole-system completeness without corresponding evidence.
